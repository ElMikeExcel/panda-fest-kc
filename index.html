What Panda Fest actually is

A traveling outdoor Asian food and culture festival. The KC event advertises 100+ food vendors, 200+ street-food offerings, 40+ merchandise vendors, live Asian cultural performances, panda-themed merchandise, a large panda inflatable and a panda bounce house.

Best mindset: this is a paid-entry food festival/market — your admission gets you inside and includes gifts/attractions, but budget separately for the food you want to try.

⚠️ The four things to know before buying

Timed entry: GA is single entry for your selected time slot. No refunds: tickets are non-refundable and the general FAQ says dates are non-transferable. Walk-up costs more: gate admission is $20 + tax and subject to capacity. Food costs extra: admission provides access to vendors; it is not an all-you-can-eat ticket.

Should you pay for VIP?

VIP makes the most sense if shorter entry lines, dedicated bathrooms and a dedicated area matter to you. If your priority is trying as much food as possible, GA leaves an extra $21 per person in your food budget on Friday/Sunday.

🍜 Food

The KC page advertises 100+ Asian food vendors and 200+ street-food items. Panda Fest describes its broader food lineup as spanning cuisines such as Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai and Vietnamese. Local event coverage specifically mentions examples such as tanghulu, dumplings and mochi donuts.

Dietary needs: Panda Fest's FAQ says many vendors offer vegetarian and vegan choices. A complete KC vendor/menu roster was not published in the sources checked, so don't assume a specific vendor or allergen-safe option will be there.

Parking warning

Panda Fest says it is not affiliated with the parking facilities, rideshare areas or private lots. Follow event signage, watch for fees/no-parking zones and don't leave valuables visible. The KC event page does not publish a guaranteed parking price.

✅ Tap-to-pack checklist

🚫 Leave these at home

Outside food/drinks (except listed exceptions), weapons of any kind, pepper spray/self-defense sprays, roller skates/skateboards, illegal drugs, and smoking/vaping products. Medical dietary items and baby formula/milk are allowed; the KC page also allows one sealed water bottle up to 16.9 oz per guest.

👨‍👩‍👧 Kids & pets

Kids: age 6+ need a ticket; under 6 enter free and receive a headband (no pin). Strollers are allowed.

KC pet rule: the KC event page specifically says well-behaved, leashed pets are welcome and owners must clean up after them.

🌧️ Weather

Panda Fest is generally rain or shine unless severe weather causes a delay, postponement or cancellation. Because the event is outdoors, check the forecast and event updates before leaving.

FAQ

Can I just show up whenever I want?

Your ticket is tied to a selected time slot. The current official general FAQ says attendees should arrive in their slot; in emergencies organizers will try to accommodate, but don't plan around late arrival.

Can I switch my ticket to another day?

The current official Panda Fest FAQ says tickets are non-transferable to other dates. Tickets are also non-refundable.

Is there seating?

The general FAQ says picnic tables are first-come, first-served. VIP includes access to dedicated seating/covered areas and VIP restrooms.

Can I bring food or drinks?

Generally no. KC exceptions: medical dietary items, baby formula/milk, and one factory-sealed water bottle up to 16.9 oz per guest.

Cash or card?

The general FAQ says most vendors accept both. There are no ATMs on-site, so carrying some cash is sensible.

Is alcohol available?

Yes, Panda Fest's FAQ says alcohol is sold to guests 21+ with valid ID.

Can I bring a bag?

Small bags are allowed, and bag checks may occur at entry.

Is it pet friendly?

For Kansas City specifically, yes: the official KC page says well-behaved leashed pets are welcome.

Can I buy a ticket at the gate?

Yes, but official information lists gate admission at $20 (the KC page says $20 + tax), subject to venue capacity, with advance ticket holders receiving priority.
